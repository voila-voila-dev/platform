New `locale` and `location` bricks. `locale` has the ISO `Locale` and `Language` lists and `SupportedLocales.make` for the locales an app renders. `location` has addresses, coordinates, country codes, the `LocationProvider` port with `locateAddress` and `resolveAddressCoordinates`, and `SupportedCountries.make` for an app's markets.
