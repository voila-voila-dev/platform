New `search-engine` brick: the `SearchEngine` port for web searches, from a country and in a locale's language, an in-memory engine with synthetic results and a stub in `/search-engine/memory`, and a DataForSEO engine in `/search-engine/dataforseo`, which maps the country and the locale to its own location and language codes. `/location` adds `numericCountryCode`.
